## User outcome

In Sources & files, read what is connected and what was actually checked, with
last attempt/success kept separate. A calm source list exposes failures, missing
catalogue entries, unsupported streams, paused settings and unknown checks.
Keep the opening dossier a document. Fold technical provenance and retain links
to original material and existing monitoring/research controls.

## Boundaries

One read-only shared dossier coverage endpoint, authorized with current dossier
roles including guests and current membership. Compose retained page state,
current topic revisions and selected source-pack operational state, plus a compact
recurring-search policy/latest-run summary. Draft choices remain planned, missing
topics remain visible, and shared feed collection is not a dossier scan or proof
that a particular query checked all sources. Preserve unavailable selected packs
and unknown streams. Do not expose global diagnostic text or unrelated workspace
records. No fetching, inference, subscriptions, writes, new tables or migrations.
Public projection is unchanged; private metadata cannot enter public dossiers.

## Acceptance

1. Both products share the same endpoint and readable component; refresh is
   explicit and never starts a scan. Loading/failure/empty are distinct; revoked
   and superseded reads cannot leave stale private content visible.
2. Failed or delayed attempts preserve the last real success; fixtures do not
   become successful live checks. Missing/inactive packs and unsupported stream
   identifiers remain visible, with known catalogue limits available.
3. Current topic status/revision is distinct from shared source health. Draft,
   paused, missing and empty selections cannot claim active or complete coverage.
   Shared success, page success and a finished search remain different facts.
4. Anonymous, cross-product, foreign-workspace and revoked guest access fail;
   read-only roles can inspect their dossier without gaining native access.
5. Meaningful API/read-only/privacy and rendered states pass, exact API Ruff,
   affected regression, both complete client test/lint/type/build gates and
   backlog invariant pass. Publish exact tested main sources and existing Sites
   artifacts; verify normal native activation and production asset hashes.
6. Full historical per-scan manifests, source/version migration, Market Access
   proving journey and human usability acceptance remain OPEN. No browser,
   private production records or paid research probes in this heartbeat.

## Implementation evidence

`product_coverage.py` reads the authenticated dossier through the existing current
principal/role gate, reuses page-watch status and resolves each current topic
revision. It does not borrow an earlier profile source selection after revision.
Missing topic references remain explicit. Selected source packs are deduplicated
and read through the existing scalar-query topic snapshot in batches of twenty.
The snapshot retains inactive and missing definitions, unsupported stream IDs,
known source gaps and last attempt/success independently. Global diagnostic text,
cursors and provider credentials are never hydrated for this response.

The recurring-search summary reads only its existing policy and latest trigger/run.
A scheduler check is explicitly separate from source retrieval. A previous run
keeps its policy revision and cannot imply that current settings were executed.
No write, job enqueue, retrieval or inference is performed by reading coverage.
Public projections remain unchanged. No schema migration is needed.

Both clients use the same source reader in Sources & files, leaving the opening
chapter unchanged. Three folded groups separate watched pages, shared collection
and recurring research; source detail and known gaps stay folded. Original page
links are validated and labels are escaped. Current-resource/session fences clear
failed or superseded reads. Navigation opens the existing Monitoring chapter or
the exact retained investigation without starting a new one.
