## Implemented contract

New exploratory episodes opt into read-relevance/v1. The existing extraction
schema gains an optional assessment with exact source/question IDs, quote/locator,
category, concise reason and typed limitations. Citation/input/version validation
precedes claim writes and assessment persistence. Missing output stays unassessed;
invalid output fails the extraction step without manufacturing an assessment.
An unrelated assessment inconsistent with extracted claims/entities/relationships
is rejected. An incomplete-reading limitation cannot open a replacement slot. Known reader
truncation is supplied explicitly and always retained as an incomplete-reading
limit, even if the model omits it; it cannot open a replacement slot either.

Assessments and replacement eligibility live in the existing branch checkpoint.
After the current captures are processed, each validated unrelated capture can
allow one further already retrieved candidate, through the existing gates and
budgets. Captures, failed attempts and citations are retained; context and
counterevidence never open that slot. No new query, model phase or schema.

The new extraction path filters claim context to wholly current permitted public
source dependencies. Supplied public dependencies are pinned before and after
inference, retained on the episode and rechecked before future work/projection.
Changed or revoked dependencies stop derived work and hide its assessment/scope.
Legacy and other noneligible paths keep their existing extraction contract.

Both clients add one compact summary, with per-question AI reasons, limitations,
exact quotes and source links inside existing closed scope details. History starts
no work. A classification is not truth, authority, professional applicability or
human review; unchanged evidence remains saved and no-claim sources can be useful.
Source-recovery and read-relevance counts describe observed paths and must not be
summed as independent work. Wider professional/semantic acceptance remains open.
