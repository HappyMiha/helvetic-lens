## Implemented contract

New episodes opt into question-assessment-renewal/v1. The final request receives
at most three server-selected question bindings: outdated assessments first, then
incomplete assessments with additional read sources, ordered by existing priority.
A source-count change only makes a question eligible for review; it never chooses
its answer status. Current claims are restricted to wholly public supplied sources,
and read-context assessments retain exact question/version/quote associations.

Optional question_renewals compose with the existing selected-question answer
schema. All bindings, full supplied context and all renewal items are checked
before any history or assessment write. Citation roles, unused/planned queries,
proposal conflicts and exact question identity reuse the existing validators.
The prior sealed checkpoint remains unchanged in branch_assessment_history; the
current receipt records its preceding fingerprint, revision and final-brief stage.
Internal records are absent from public questions, plans and model context.

The final briefing keeps its complete internal public read/claim/source snapshot,
even when no optional renewal is returned. Current rights, hashes, claim changes
and read-context changes withhold dependent output and continuation. Typed ancestry
reuses that fence. Eligible earlier checkpoints remain explicitly historical;
source or private-backing changes cannot expose them through the reader.

Both clients reuse the existing closed question details and citation renderer.
The current checkpoint is marked Updated in the research summary; Earlier
assessment opens the old cited interpretation without another question heading,
form, control or automatic request. Existing typed child outcomes remain separate.
Local checks passed; production activation remains pending.
