# A question becomes a researched dossier

Owner request, 1 October 2026. Implemented and locally verified; activation is recorded separately.
Scope: MV2-020/023 research, MV2-022 meaningful updates, shared Legal/Pharma reader.
Depends on the activated source/memory/update contracts documented in
[the preceding release](RESEARCH_SOURCES_MEMORY_AND_UPDATES.md), existing native
jobs, source rights, exact citations, review policy and explicit public-query consent.

## Outcome and acceptance

One vague, difficult question starts a durable research mission. The system maps
the question, reads evidence, follows consequential gaps and checks contrary
evidence before presenting an answer with cited support, conflicts and named
unknowns. It retains useful checkpoints and explains why work continues or stops.
It asks a question only when a choice changes the research. The owner need not
operate an architectural control panel. All five blocks ship as this journey:

1. Autonomous evidence-led rounds until meaningful completion; a restart
   resumes committed progress. Deduplicate queries and stop on no useful progress,
   changed rights, consequential user choice or actual provider/access failure.
2. Read large permitted documents incrementally beyond opening pages/characters,
   including OCR where available. Preserve original hashes and stable locations;
   never mix changed document versions. Show unread portions and failed extraction.
3. Build a current dossier knowledge view across runs: exact identifiers/current
   identity reviews, cited claim evolution, duplicate origins and explicit unknown
   independence. Retain historical originals and human review separately.
4. Classify actual evidence changes by their effect on the question. One explainable
   materiality projection drives immediate, digest and silent in-app following;
   unsuccessful checks remain gaps, not reassurance. No external messages in tests.
5. Extract cited professional context (legal jurisdiction/authority/version or
   medicine/indication/market/status), retain ambiguity, and use it in the answer,
   gap selection and change assessment. No conclusion from branding or missing data.

Acceptance uses a neutral fictional worker journey with a late-document passage,
cross-run knowledge, conflicting sources and an unresolved question; verifies
resumption, source changes/withdrawal, no-progress termination and delivery policy.
Both clients expose the same calm reader and simple preferences. Run affected API
tests, exact lint/backlog guard and required client gates; push main and verify
normal Core and both existing Sites deployments. No unfinished substep releases.

## Source readiness and boundaries

Public adapters, local SearXNG and approved retained material are available under
their current scope. Authenticated archives/enterprise systems need actual access;
no full-internet coverage is claimed. Free discovery keeps no application query
quota. Per-operation deadlines and parser containment remain.
Monitoring/publication consent, ACLs, human review, all nine Monitoring sections,
Apache-2.0, retired hosts and deferred directions are unchanged. No paid/live/private
research, production fixture dossiers or browser automation for validation.
Controlled fixtures establish behavior, not real-world professional answer quality.

## Whole-document completion (owner clarification, 1 October)

A 400+ page original is one document, not a succession of paid searches. Fetch and
retain its immutable bytes once; resume local page/character extraction across
worker leases without consuming source-fetch or model allowances for each portion.
Read every page before analysing that document. Assemble full-text analysis batches
without ranking away sections, retain cited section notes, and reconcile the whole
document for exceptions, contradictions and internal references. Final synthesis
uses those notes and their original quotations, never an unbounded whole-file prompt.

Extraction, section analysis and whole-document reconciliation have distinct
completion receipts. Any unread page, failed section or pending reconciliation
keeps the document incomplete and prevents a finished-research claim. Technical
failures retain exact progress for resumption, never silently skip the remainder.
There is no internal execution allowance to exhaust. Local parser containment
is a per-job boundary, not a first-pages coverage cutoff. Support 100 MB originals
and page-aware continuation through at least 400 pages; unsupported/encrypted or
unreadable material remains an explicit gap. Verify a 420-page fictional original,
late counterevidence, restart-safe progress, one original fetch, and failed OCR or
analysis preventing completion.

## Dossier allowance replaces execution budgets (final owner instruction)

Three owned dossiers per account, shared across Legal and Pharma and workspaces.
All existing dossiers count; sharing does not consume a second owner's place.
Research, continuation and technical retry within a dossier do not spend places.
Deleting a dossier frees its place. Existing dossiers above the base allowance are
retained; new creation waits for available capacity. Check creation transactionally
on every entry point, including manual creation and copies of public dossiers.

Users can request a higher total dossier limit with a brief reason. A durable email
goes to info@helveticlens.ch, linking approval, rejection and an alternative total.
The platform administrator reviews the exact account/request and confirms a POST;
opening a link or an email scanner must never change a limit. Decisions are audited,
revision-checked and idempotent. Pending requests and current limits are visible to
the requesting user; no private dossier contents enter the email.

Once admitted, research has no internal request/time/round/depth allowance: it
continues until a meaningful outcome, no useful new evidence, a consequential user
choice or an actual access/provider failure. Per-operation deadlines and parser
containment remain; recorded usage is observability, not a stop condition. This
supersedes references to paid/time execution limits in the preceding scope text.

## Implementation and acceptance evidence

The controlled Legal journey fetched one fictional 420-page original once, resumed
local reads through worker transactions, passed all 420 pages into section analysis,
and reconciled a first-page reference against the exception on page 420. The mission
continued from an initial contradiction to a second cited answer with a named gap.
Pharma uses the identical mechanism and its own product-isolated route.

Whole-document analysis failure prevents completion; explicit retry retains the
original and completed sections. Changed source hashes redact the earlier mission
answer. Current dossier knowledge includes prior permitted runs, reviewed identities,
claim evolution and unknown/duplicate source independence. Delivery-mode checks cover
quiet changes, findings, access failures and preserving unread positions.

Account-capacity checks cover mixed-product creation, existing-dossier research,
request replay, a single pending request, authenticated owner review, read-only email
links, requested/alternative approval, rejection and unchanged/revision-conflicting
decisions. Native queued email dispatch uses a captured SMTP transport in tests; no
real recipient received a fixture. Production SMTP configuration was checked read-only.

Both clients passed functional tests, lint, type checks and production builds. The
large-upload gateway starts streaming before consuming a 13 MiB fixture, exceeding
its former limit without buffering a whole original. Exact release activation and
remaining limitations are recorded in the parent research-mission checkpoint.
Controlled evidence does not establish live professional answer quality or complete
source coverage. Existing recurring monitoring schedules/standing authorizations
remain separate from the new dossier admission and completion-based research policy.
