# Refine ongoing research without losing useful work

Scope recorded before code, 1 October 2026. Implemented and locally validated.
Production activation is tracked in the parent research-refinement checkpoint.
MV2-002/020/024 and broader architecture remain OPEN.

Observed journey gap: the client pauses and replies in two separate writes; a
failed reply can leave useful work stopped. A correction in the user's own words
also ends research-memory ancestry, so the next planner starts without earlier
searches, reading or unresolved questions. Free text is unavailable before an
orientation or pause. These obstruct the universal research journey.

Deliver one explicit continue action for a suggested or user-written direction,
available while research runs. Validate then pause/link/enqueue atomically, with
replay safety and a fence against old in-flight results. Carry bounded earlier
public work into the existing planner for an explicitly linked refinement. Keep
the corrected question authoritative; historical passages are context, never new
citation authority or invented AI rationale. Preserve source/ancestry checks,
current access and separate monitoring permission. Existing callers retain their
pause/reply and free-text boundary unless they explicitly choose the new action.

Reuse native jobs, investigation records, research memory, ancestry and existing
reader controls. No new engine, provider, form, budget or monitoring permission.
Current adapters and fictional integration fixtures are ready. No paid/live/private
research or browser work. Source changes must invalidate dependent results.

actual earlier public work in the next planner, change real search, and deliver
current-source findings. One action creates one successor; stale/unauthorised
choices change nothing; lost responses replay safely; old worker output cannot
resurface. The reader supports early correction and preserves text on failure.
