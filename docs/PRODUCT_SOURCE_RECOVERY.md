## Implemented behavior

`product_source_recovery.py` adds a source-recovery/v1 opt-in and keeps replacement
selection on existing branch checkpoints. Each failed or interrupted read frees
one selection slot, bounded by the same finite candidates and unchanged global
quotas. Initial and replacement items remain in order; no failed URL is removed
and retried. Duplicate captures alone do not free a slot. New public branches
deduplicate the retrieved URL list and recheck current exclusions/attempted URLs
at gate and read boundaries; the reader retains its existing access rules.

The existing scope projection retains failed attempts, counts actual replacement
checks/reads/captures and identifies exhausted or unfinished candidate sets. The
existing final briefing receives that same observed scope. Its current activity
receipt labels actual alternative gate/read work, expiring under the 1.58 lease
and client deadlines. Both clients display one concise explanatory paragraph
alongside observed scope, including history without actions. Source and ancestry
revocation suppress dependent output. No new schema, search, recurrence or form.
